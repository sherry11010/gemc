About GEMC Global

Our Story

GEMC Global is the international summit series focused on methane reduction and renewable-gas value-chain. We organize global-scale summits connecting energy-industry stakeholders across Asia-Pacific, Europe and Middle-East.

Our events enable project matchmaking, policy discussion, technology showcase and investment dialogue for methane abatement, biogas-biomethane, SAF and low-carbon LNG markets.

Purpose, Vision & Values

Purpose

To accelerate global methane-mitigation and renewable-gas deployment, by delivering high-impact summits that spark connections, shape industry agendas and turn ideas into real-world projects.

Vision

Become the world’s most trusted conference platform for methane and renewable-gas stakeholders, bridging operators, technology vendors, financiers and policymakers.

Core Values

  • Industry-focused & Practical
  • Global & Inclusive Collaboration
  • Action-oriented Outcomes
  • Trusted Delivery
