OUR STORY

Since starting out in Singapore, GEMC Global has hosted high-level methane and renewable-gas transition events across Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Europe.

From sparking new ideas and amplifying brand voices to connecting business partners and facilitating key deals; from exploring emerging markets to witnessing projects come to life — GEMC Global stands with the industry. We are here for every exploration and every challenge, providing reliable industry platforms.

We are the catalyst for progress, the stage for innovation, and the go-to place for networking, showcasing, and stimulating growth. Join us as we accelerate the global clean gas transition.

OUR PURPOSE, VISION AND VALUES

OUR PURPOSE

To accelerate the global methane abatement and renewable-gas deployment by designing impactful summits that ignite meaningful connections, shape industry agendas, and turn conversations into commercial progress, bringing us closer to a sustainable low-carbon gas future.

OUR VISION

To redefine the future of methane & renewable-gas industry events as the world’s most dynamic, trusted platform for innovation, where operators, investors, technologists and policymakers converge to turn ideas into real-world impact.

OUR VALUES

Passion | Curious & Bold | Collaborative | Trusted to Deliver
We fuel the clean gas transition with passion. Bold and curious, we explore technical frontiers. We connect cross-border stakeholders, and we deliver real, actionable outcomes.
