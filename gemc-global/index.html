GEMC Global — Where the Industry Connects and Progresses.

About Us

Since starting out in Shanghai, GEMC Global has hosted high-level methane and renewable-gas transition events across Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Europe.

From sparking new ideas and amplifying brand voices to connecting business partners and facilitating key deals; from exploring emerging markets to witnessing projects come to life — GEMC Global stands with the industry. We are here for every exploration and every challenge, providing reliable industry platforms.

We are the catalyst for progress, the stage for innovation, and the go-to place for networking, showcasing, and stimulating growth. Join us as we accelerate the global clean gas transition.

OUR PURPOSE, VISION AND VALUES

OUR PURPOSE

GEMC Global exists to provide an open, pragmatic and trustworthy platform for dialogue and cooperation—where nations, industries and perspectives meet, where consensus takes shape, and where collaboration becomes reality. We are more than a summit: we turn connection into action, and action into real and lasting value for the world.

OUR VISION

Our vision is a GEMC Global where every gathering becomes actionable cooperation, and every partnership stands the test of time and responsibility—where global partners move forward together on a path of innovation and sustainability, shaping a more open, resilient and prosperous future.

OUR VALUES

Collaboration · Innovation · Integrity

GEMC Global is driven by a mission to connect global partners and advance pragmatic cooperation. The five values below guide how we convene, how we collaborate, and how we take responsibility for the world.
