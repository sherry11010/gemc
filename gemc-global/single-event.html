GEMC Global 2026

Global Energy & Methane Conference

Event Overview

GEMC Global 2026 is the flagship summit for methane-reduction and renewable-gas industries. It brings together energy operators, technology suppliers, investors, carbon-verifiers and regulators.

Key themes: methane monitoring & abatement, biogas-biomethane project development, SAF feedstock & commercialization, low-carbon-LNG, carbon-credit & ESG financing.
